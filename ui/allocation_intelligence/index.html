Why might Strategic Targets show PASS while Current Portfolio shows OVER?
Strategic Target Compliance validates the allocation planning model. It checks whether the intended target percentages are within policy limits. Current Portfolio Compliance evaluates your actual holdings today. The difference between them is allocation drift — natural movement as market prices change and trades occur. Both indicators are correct; they measure different things.

Architecture & Philosophy

  • Asset-class-first hierarchy — EQUITIES / FIXED_INCOME / DIGITAL / COMMODITIES / CASH are Level 1.
  • Dot-notation keys — e.g. EQUITIES.US.MEGA.HYPER_MEGA
  • Propagation formula — target_pct_of_total = ∏(target_pct_of_parent across ancestry chain)
  • Replay sophistication — HIGH (equity nodes), LOW (fixed income), NONE (digital/commodities/cash)
  • Semi-automated recalculation — engine proposes, human runs --commit
  • Sum invariant — each parent's children sum to 100.0 ±0.01
  • 8 validators — hierarchy_sums, policy_bounds, tactical_overflow, overlay_staleness, recalculation_churn, evidence_alignment, concentration_ceilings, lineage_completeness
