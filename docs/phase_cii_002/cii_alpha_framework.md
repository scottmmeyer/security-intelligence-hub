# CII Alpha Framework — Phase CII-002

## Expected Sources of Alpha

Four alpha sources are now explicitly documented in the CII methodology panel.

### 1. Consensus Intelligence
**Definition:** Leverages the collective research and judgment of professional analysts rather than relying on individual opinion.

**Alpha mechanism:** Professional analysts, in aggregate, have superior access to company management, industry contacts, and proprietary data. An individual investor cannot match this information advantage on any individual security. By using aggregated consensus (ESS + Zacks + Danelfin), CII captures the collective edge of hundreds of analyst-years of research.

**Why this generates alpha:** Consensus signals have historically shown predictive value at the aggregate level, particularly when multiple independent sources converge. The signal-to-noise ratio improves when ESS, Zacks, and Danelfin AI all point in the same direction.

---

### 2. Fundamental Confirmation
**Definition:** Validates whether business performance supports the prevailing market narrative, reducing exposure to deteriorating theses.

**Alpha mechanism:** Analyst ratings can lag fundamental deterioration by 1–4 quarters. By cross-checking consensus against FMP fundamental data (revenue growth, ROIC, beat rate), CII identifies positions where the narrative has outrun the business — potential value traps — before they damage the portfolio.

**Why this generates alpha:** Avoiding value traps is a primary source of alpha in any concentrated portfolio. The Thesis Integrity classifier (INTACT / QUESTIONABLE / DETERIORATING) systematizes what experienced investors do intuitively.

---

### 3. Historical Validation
**Definition:** Requires empirical evidence that similar signal configurations have historically produced favorable outcomes.

**Alpha mechanism:** The Replay system requires that every deployment candidate has appeared in historical winning portfolio configurations. This prevents "this time it's different" positioning and anchors recommendations in empirical evidence rather than pure narrative.

**Why this generates alpha:** Eliminates narrative-only positions. Every deployment candidate has a historical precedent — which removes a class of low-quality ideas that look compelling on current data but have no track record.

---

### 4. Portfolio Construction Discipline
**Definition:** Directs capital toward the highest-conviction opportunities while managing concentration and allocation risk.

**Alpha mechanism:** Even superior security selection can be destroyed by poor sizing, overconcentration, or allocation drift. CW-DAS ensures that capital flows to the highest-conviction candidates while respecting position limits (6% WARN, 8% MAX), allocation node targets, and mandate constraints.

**Why this generates alpha:** Behavioral finance research consistently shows that position sizing and portfolio construction are the primary destroyers of individual investor alpha. Disciplined construction preserves the gains from superior selection.

---

## Summary

CII generates expected alpha through four compounding advantages:
1. Collective intelligence > individual opinion
2. Fundamental validation catches stale narratives
3. Historical evidence gates out unproven ideas
4. Portfolio discipline prevents self-inflicted losses

No single source is sufficient alone. The combination creates a framework where each layer validates the others.
