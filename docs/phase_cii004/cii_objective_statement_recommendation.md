# CII Objective Statement Recommendation — Phase CII-004

## Current Objective Statement (Modal)

> "Identify and allocate capital toward opportunities where analyst consensus, business fundamentals, historical evidence, and portfolio discipline align most favorably in pursuit of superior long-term risk-adjusted returns."

**Assessment:** Accurate but incomplete. The statement correctly identifies the four input types but does not convey:
1. That fundamentals now actively adjust the conviction score (not just display)
2. That the system reduces errors (avoids deteriorating theses) as an explicit alpha mechanism
3. That ranking reflects both opportunity identification AND error reduction

---

## Current Statement (Methodology Documentation)

> "Consensus Intelligence Investing begins with professional analyst consensus, validates that consensus against business fundamentals and historical evidence, scores opportunities using an internal conviction framework, and deploys capital through a risk-managed model designed to maximize long-term portfolio growth."

**Assessment:** Partially outdated. "Validates against business fundamentals" correctly describes the intent, but post-ISSUE-07 the statement should convey that this validation now adjusts the scoring, not just informs display.

---

## Recommended Objective Statement (Modal)

> "Identify and allocate capital toward high-conviction opportunities where analyst consensus, business fundamentals, historical evidence, and portfolio discipline align most favorably — while systematically reducing allocation errors where consensus has outrun business reality — in pursuit of superior long-term risk-adjusted returns."

**What changed:** Added "while systematically reducing allocation errors where consensus has outrun business reality." This directly references ISSUE-07's error-reduction function (PSX case: DETERIORATING thesis while consensus remains BULLISH).

---

## Recommended Main Statement (Documentation + Modal)

> "Consensus Intelligence Investing (CII) is a portfolio construction methodology that begins with professional analyst consensus, validates that consensus against business fundamentals and historical evidence — allowing fundamental quality to adjust conviction scores — and deploys capital through a risk-managed framework designed to generate superior long-term risk-adjusted returns."

**What changed:** Added "allowing fundamental quality to adjust conviction scores" — accurately describes CW-DAS v1.1 where the fundamental modifier is an active component, not a display label.

---

## Principle: Minimum Effective Change

The recommended changes are the minimum required to accurately represent the certified architecture. They do not:
- Change the primacy of analyst consensus
- Introduce new philosophical concepts
- Remove or diminish any existing philosophy element
- Imply autonomous decision-making

The additions simply make explicit what was already intended by the phrase "validates against fundamentals" — that validation has consequences for ranking, not just display.
