## Top 3 Recommendations

### Recommendation 1 (Primary): "Where Analyst Consensus Meets Portfolio Discipline"

**Why:** This tagline captures the two things that most distinguish SIH from other investment approaches — (1) it starts from professional analyst consensus rather than personal opinion, and (2) it subjects that consensus to portfolio discipline rather than acting on it indiscriminately. It is professional, accurate, and explanatory without requiring additional context.

**Best for:** Header subtitle, institutional marketing, advisor conversations, documentation headers.

---

### Recommendation 2 (Concise / Product): "Consensus Validated. Conviction Built. Capital Deployed."

**Why:** The three-clause structure is memorable and mirrors the methodology's three active phases (signal → validation → deployment). Each clause is active-verb-led, communicating a system that does something rather than just describes something. Easy to say aloud.

**Best for:** Marketing materials, presentations, exported report cover pages, product onboarding.

---

### Recommendation 3 (Plain Language / Accessible): "Signals Tell You What. Fundamentals Tell You Why."

**Why:** Immediately accessible to a non-technical audience. Anyone who has ever heard an analyst recommendation and wondered "but is the business actually good?" will understand this instantly. It explains the methodology in two sentences that could lead any investor education piece.

**Best for:** Consumer-facing materials, advisor explanations, onboarding copy, UI help text.
