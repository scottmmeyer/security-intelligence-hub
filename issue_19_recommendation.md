# ISSUE-19 Final Recommendation

Project: Security Intelligence Hub (SIH)  
Assessment: Fund Vehicle Intelligence (FVI)  
Date: 2026-06-06

## Executive Decision

FVI should be formalized as ISSUE-19 and treated as a governance-priority capability gap: SIH needs vehicle-quality intelligence to complement existing sleeve-allocation intelligence.

## Required Final Recommendation Answers

1. Should ISSUE-19 exist?
- Yes. The gap is real, recurring, and currently affects CRA/Allocation Reduction decision quality.

2. What priority should it receive?
- High priority in assessment-to-design queue, but implementation should remain staged and governance-gated.

3. Is it more valuable than MCI?
- Near-term operational value: yes, for portfolio implementation quality and replacement discipline.
- Long-term strategic breadth: MCI remains important, but FVI has more immediate decision-quality impact on fund-heavy portfolios.

4. Should it influence CRA?
- Yes, initially as advisory context and later as a replacement-policy gate.
- No direct score mutation in initial phases.

5. Should it influence allocation reduction recommendations?
- Yes, by separating sleeve action from vehicle action.
- FVI should prevent low-evidence replacement of high-quality vehicles when a sleeve is merely overweight.

6. What is the minimum implementation worth building?
- Advisory Fund Quality Score with LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/ELITE bands using peer-relative risk-adjusted return, downside behavior, and cost percentile.

## Implementation Staging Recommendation (Governance Only)

Stage 1: Advisory FVI
- Peer-normalized quality labels
- Explainable factor breakdown
- No automatic replacement

Stage 2: Recommendation Gate
- Replacement suggestions allowed only with friction-adjusted net-benefit evidence
- Human confirmation required

Stage 3: Expanded Influence (optional)
- Consider deeper CRA/PAP integration only after multi-quarter evidence and governance approval

## Priority Positioning in Current Roadmap

Recommended ordering:
1. ISSUE-12D evidence program (already open and blocked by maturity window)
2. Governance tooling and evidence infrastructure
3. ISSUE-19 FVI advisory capability assessment-to-design
4. CRA/PAP follow-on enhancements
5. MCI follow-on implementation planning

## Risk Statement

Primary risk if ISSUE-19 is not pursued:
- continued conflation of sleeve pressure with vehicle quality, increasing false replacement risk and avoidable switching friction.

Primary risk if pursued without governance controls:
- overconfident replacement actions from weak evidence.

## Governance Conditions for ISSUE-19

- No active-vs-passive bias assumptions.
- No replacement recommendation without explicit evidence and friction-adjusted economics.
- Every recommendation must be explainable and auditable.
- Human override authority remains mandatory.

## Final Outcome

ISSUE-19 is recommended as a governed, high-value assessment/design track with phased influence: advisory first, policy-gated recommendation influence second, and no direct score methodology changes unless future evidence meets elevated governance standards.
