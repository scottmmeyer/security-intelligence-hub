## Q1 — Is DODFX Actually a Good Vehicle?

**Yes. DODFX is a high-quality active international fund.**

Evidence from public knowledge (Dodge & Cox International as of 2026):

**Performance:** Dodge & Cox International has historically delivered strong long-term risk-adjusted returns versus its Foreign Large Value peer group. The fund has a consistent record of outperforming its Morningstar category over 5+ year periods through value-oriented stock selection.

**Manager Quality:** Dodge & Cox employs a team-based investment management approach with very long average tenure. Manager team stability is significantly above the active fund industry average. The investment process has been essentially unchanged for decades.

**Expense Ratio:** ~0.63% (Class I shares). This is below the active international fund peer median (~1.1–1.3%) and notably low for an active foreign large value manager of this quality.

**Load:** DODFX Class I shares have no front-end load. The operator SELL_LAST policy was set appropriately — if there were a front-end load already paid, it would be a sunk cost, but for DODFX there is no load friction.

**Consistency:** The fund has avoided the style drift that undermines many active managers. It has maintained its value orientation through multiple market cycles.
