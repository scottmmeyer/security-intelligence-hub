## Snapshot Coverage Caveat

All classification is based on **10 universe snapshots** spanning May 2025 to June 2026 (approximately 13 months with significant gaps). The gap between May 2025 and May 2026 is ~12 months with no intermediate universe snapshots — meaning persistence rates reflect the bookends of a long window rather than continuous monitoring.

**Implication**: A symbol rated A_PERSISTENT_LEADER is confirmed to have maintained top-decile status at both the earliest and most recent observations, but the intermediate path is inferred, not directly observed.

**Improvement path**: Daily or weekly universe snapshots would yield a far more granular classification. ESS archive (34 snapshots, near-daily in recent months) provides supplementary signal continuity data for the ESS dimension specifically.
