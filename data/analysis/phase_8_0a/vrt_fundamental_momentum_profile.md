## Business Description

Vertiv designs, manufactures, and services critical digital infrastructure: power management systems (UPS, PDUs, switchgear), thermal cooling (precision air conditioning, liquid cooling), and IT infrastructure management (DCIM, remote monitoring). Products are the backbone of data center power and cooling.

**AI Tailwind**: VRT is a direct beneficiary of the global data center buildout driven by AI workloads. Every hyperscale AI cluster requires VRT-category power management and thermal infrastructure.
