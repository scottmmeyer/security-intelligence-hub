## 1. Overview

The Fundamental Momentum Score (FMS) is a 100-point composite metric computed from five sub-components:
- **RMS (25 pts)**: Revenue Momentum Score — growth rate, acceleration, forward guidance uplift
- **EMS (20 pts)**: EPS Momentum Score — earnings trajectory, sustainability, beat/miss pattern
- **ERS (20 pts)**: Estimate Revision Score — analyst estimate direction, PT changes
- **EQS (15 pts)**: Earnings Quality Score — FCF/NI ratio, FCF margin trend, gross margin trend
- **VRS (10 pts)**: Valuation Reasonableness Score — PEG-based; premium penalized at extremes
- **GTS (10 pts)**: Guidance Trend Score — forward guidance, beat rate, raised/maintained pattern

This report computes estimated FMS for 28 symbols where sufficient fundamental data is available and evaluates correlations with SIH signal components (ESS, Zacks, composite score, persistence score).
