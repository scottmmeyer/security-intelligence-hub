## 6. Business Model Context

Arrow Electronics is the world's second-largest electronic components distributor (after Avnet), serving:
- Industrial automation customers
- Aerospace/defense supply chains
- IoT and embedded computing OEMs
- EV/auto electronics

### Why the 2023 Trough Occurred
- Post-COVID inventory overbuild in 2021-2022: customers ordered excessively to protect against supply shortages
- By 2023: customers drew down excess inventory rather than placing new orders
- This "inventory correction" cycle is a recurring pattern in electronics distribution (occurs every 5-8 years)
- Prior cycles: 2001-2002, 2008-2009, 2015-2016, 2023-2024

### Why This Recovery is Legitimate
1. Inventory levels have normalized — end customers rebuilding
2. AI infrastructure buildout creating structural demand for embedded processors, power components
3. EV/auto electronics content increasing per vehicle
4. Industrial automation resuming capital spending
5. Revenue +20.5% TTM — confirmed order book expansion, not just price
