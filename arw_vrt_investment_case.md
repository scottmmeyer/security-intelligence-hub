### The concentrated alpha investment logic

A concentrated alpha portfolio is built around a small number of high-conviction positions that are allowed to become large. The logic for continuing to add to VRT is:

1. **Prior commitment is evidence of conviction.** The fact that VRT represents 3.60% of the portfolio means previous deployment rounds — and every trim review since — have affirmed VRT as a core holding.

2. **Compounding conviction.** In a concentrated alpha strategy, the value is not in finding the next best signal, but in compounding the positions that have already been established as core. Adding to ARW starts a new position build. Adding to VRT reinforces an established conviction.

3. **Position size creates exit optionality.** A larger VRT position gives the PM more flexibility to trim strategically as conditions change, creating a two-sided capital management tool.
